# Costa Rica

Status: **Structure and research plan only.** Every concept is `UNRESEARCHED` and `NOT IMPLEMENTED`. This directory contains no legal rules.

Costa Rica is PalCheck's first localization target ([LATAM_STRATEGY.md](../LATAM_STRATEGY.md)). "First" means it is the only country studied in depth; it does not
make Costa Rica's structures a template for other countries.

## What is here

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md) | Native terms named in the brief, with neutral orientation and per-concept status. Orientation only; not authoritative. |
| [LEGAL_RESEARCH_POLICY.md](LEGAL_RESEARCH_POLICY.md) | How law is researched, cited, verified, and reviewed before anything is shown as authoritative or implemented as behavior. |
| [RESEARCH_BACKLOG.md](RESEARCH_BACKLOG.md) | The questions to answer, by topic and priority. Questions only; no answers. |
| `sources/` | **Does not exist yet.** Created when the first source is verified, to hold citations and (where permitted) snapshots. |

## Scope

In scope for Phase 0: naming the concepts; separating domain, legal, and software layers; listing research questions; defining how research will be done.

Out of scope until concepts are `VERIFIED` and professionally reviewed: any statement of what the law requires; any percentage, formula, deadline, or eligibility rule;
any calculation; any compliance claim.

## How this connects to the product

- Concepts will appear in the Legal Concept Registry ([../LEGAL_CONCEPT_REGISTRY.md](../LEGAL_CONCEPT_REGISTRY.md)) as `Concept` entries at `UNRESEARCHED`.
- In Phase 1 they are shown as a read-only "Learn" glossary with honest status banners.
- Research answers about *data shape* (worker types, identifiers, employer structures) come first, because they decide the domain model. Answers about *computation*
  come much later, if ever ([DOMAIN_MODEL.md](../../DOMAIN_MODEL.md) section 12).
